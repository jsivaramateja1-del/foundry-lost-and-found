    Process

    Three steps, no fuss

    01

    Report it

    Lost something or found something? Post the details — what it is, roughly where, and when.

    02

    Search for a match

    Filter by category, location and status. Foundry also flags listings that look like yours.

    03

    Collect it safely

    Send a claim with a detail only the owner would know. Once it's approved, arrange the handover.

    Community

    Built to sit beside your class groups

    Foundry doesn't replace the WhatsApp groups you already use — it gives them somewhere to point. Instead of a lost-item photo scrolling out of view by evening, it lives in one searchable place with a status, a location and a date attached.

    Sharing to WhatsApp — implemented, one listing at a time Every listing has a share button that opens WhatsApp with the item pre-filled, so you can drop it into your class group yourself. What this build does not do is post into a group automatically: it does not connect to any group, read any messages, or send anything on its own.
    Claims are reviewed by the person who posted the item Not by university staff. There is no verification of ownership beyond the description you provide, and the finder is emailed so they know to look.
