Process

Three steps, no fuss

01

Report it

Lost something or found something? Post the details — what it is, roughly where, and when.

02

Search for a match

Filter by category, location and status. Foundry also flags listings that look like yours.

03

Collect it safely

Send a claim with a detail only the owner would know. Once it's approved, arrange the handover.

Community

Built to sit beside your class groups

Foundry doesn't replace the WhatsApp groups you already use — it gives them somewhere to point. Instead of a lost-item photo scrolling out of view by evening, it lives in one searchable place with a status, a location and a date attached.

WhatsApp integration — not implemented A future version could post new listings into a campus group automatically. This build does not connect to any group, read any messages, or send anything. Any such button here would be a placeholder only.
Claims are reviewed by the person who posted the item Not by university staff. There is no verification of ownership beyond the description you provide, and no notification is emailed to anyone.
